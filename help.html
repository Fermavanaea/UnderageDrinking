How to Overcome Alcohol

WHAT IS UNDERAGE DRINKING?

Underage drinking is when a person drinks alcohol before reaching the legal drinking age. It can be harmful because alcohol can affect a young person’s developing brain and lead to poor decisions, accidents, and health problems. It can also affect school, relationships, and future wellbeing. For these reasons, it is important for young people to understand the risks of drinking alcohol and make safe and responsible choices. Underage drinking is a series issue that affects the health, safety, and future of New Zealand’s young people. Our goal is to raise awareness, provide facts, and empower teens to make better choices for a better tomorrow.
