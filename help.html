How to Overcome Alcohol

HOW TO SAY NO TO ALCOHOL.

Being offered alcohol can sometimes feel uncomfortable, especially when you feel pressured by friends or people around you. Saying no doesn't have to be awkward or difficult.

Easy ways to say no

  • "No thanks, I'm good."
  • "Nah, I don't drink."
  • "I'm not interested."
  • "I'm happy with what I'm drinking."
  • "No, I'm going to pass."
