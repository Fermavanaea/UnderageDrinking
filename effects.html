Effects of Alcohol

Impulse control?

Alcohol harms the frontal lobe and prefrontal cortex, which control judgment, planning, and self-control.

Memory and learning:

It damages the hippocampus, making it hard to learn new things, retain information, or recall memories.

blackouts

Teens experience alcohol-induced blackouts more easily than adults, losing chunks of memory while awake.

Mental Health and Behavior

  • Mental health:

    Early drinking raises the risk of long-term anxiety and depression.
  • Addiction: Starting young makes a person significantly more likely to develop an alcohol use disorder later in life.
  • Risky actions: Teens are more likely to engage in unsafe sex, physical fights, or risky behaviors under the influence
